ABOUT US

Since 2010, we have provided high-quality Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu training in a supportive and inclusive environment.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Every class at Focus BJJ combines clear technical instruction, focused drilling and live rounds that help students apply what they have learned. Beginners build strong fundamentals at their own pace, while experienced grapplers receive the detail and individual feedback needed to keep progressing.

OUR COACHES

Led by Head Coach Rickson Tavares, every session is built around clear instruction, careful attention to technique and a safe training environment. Rickson works closely with students at every level, helping beginners gain confidence while challenging experienced grapplers to sharpen the details of their game.
