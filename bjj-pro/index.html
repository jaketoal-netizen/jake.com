04 / BEFORE YOU START

NEW TO THIS?
YOU’RE IN
GOOD COMPANY.

Everyone remembers their first class.
Here’s what to know before yours.

Do I need any experience?+

None at all. Start with a beginner session, where you’ll learn the basic movements and positions at a comfortable pace. Tell your coach it’s your first visit.

What should I bring?+

Bring water, a towel, and clean training clothes without zips or pockets. For a Gi class, ask us about kit before coming along.

Do I need to get fit first?+

You can build fitness as you train. Take breaks when you need them and let your coach know about any concerns before the session.

What is the difference between Gi and No Gi?+

Gi classes use a traditional training uniform, including jacket grips. No Gi uses close-fitting sportswear and a different approach to grips and control.

How do I arrange my first class?+

Use the trial planner below to choose a session and see what to bring. Contact the academy directly to confirm availability before attending.
